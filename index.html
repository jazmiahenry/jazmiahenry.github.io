Look 01 — Atelier piece

RIGGs · Collide

2025 — present · Principal MTS, AI/ML

Full ownership of the RIGGs platform. miniRIGGs — an 8B model trained via simulation-driven fine-tuning — completes the standardized PE exam in fifteen minutes versus Grok-4's two hours and scores 35% against the Llama 3.1 baseline's 18%. BigRIGGs — 120B MoE on a 55B-token domain corpus — ships with a 16-dimensional reward system, simulation environments for retrieval, routing, and long-context reasoning, and a multimodal embedding stack pairing SigLIP with EVA02-CLIP under a four-stage curriculum. Sub-100ms response on the MoE at production load via sparse expert routing.
